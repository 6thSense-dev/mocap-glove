6thSense V6 diagnostics 0.2 — 24 October 2026

STATUS
Built and software-tested. Not yet flashed or electrically validated on your hub.
This release provides component tests, not calibrated motion tracking.
Keep the original firmware-app folder as your 0.1 fallback.

START
Open control-panel/Start-control-panel.command and http://127.0.0.1:8775.
Only one panel/server should own the hub. Close the old panel before flashing.
The panel accepts the old 0.1.0-bench image for existing tests, but enables the
new controls only for the exact 0.2.0-diagnostics firmware identity.
Connecting USB/panel does not enable the sensor rail or transmitter.

FLASH
Disconnect USB and unplug coil harness and finger flexes. Reconnect USB.
Hold BOOT, tap/release RESET, release BOOT.
Run firmware-app/host/Flash-hub-Mac.command. It backs up all main flash,
programs only application flash, and verifies byte-for-byte readback.
The new binary is firmware-app/build/glove-v6-diagnostics.bin.
Do not change option bytes. After flashing release BOOT and tap RESET.
Connect panel: expect firmware 0.2.0-diagnostics and sensor power Off.

TEST ORDER
1. Hub alone: connect, enable sensor rail, read current sensors.
   Expect a valid supply reading near 3.3 V and three raw current channels
   near 2048/4095 (half the rail). Record actual values, not a guessed pass.
2. Check IMU, Enable IMU, Read IMU. BMI323 identity 0x43; accelerometer
   +/-8 g and gyro +/-2000 deg/s, 100 Hz. Raw accelerometer /4096 gives g;
   raw gyro *2000/32768 gives deg/s. Temperature = raw/512 + 23 C.
   Move board and verify changing readings. Off disables accel and gyro.
3. Power off/unplug USB, connect one finger at J1, reconnect. Power on,
   Check fingertips, Read selected, Capture selected. Repeat each port.
   CRC detects frame corruption, not physical correctness or sample continuity.
4. With USB unplugged, measure resistance of your wound coil. Check no
   short to neighbouring pins, then connect only that coil to J9:
   X pins 1-2, Y pins 3-4, Z pins 5-6. Confirm physical pin 1 on the PCB;
   do not infer it from left/right in a rotated photograph.
5. Connect grounded oscilloscope ground clips ONLY to board GND (J7 GND
   pins 3/6, after confirming orientation). Both coil terminals are driven.
   Never ground a coil terminal with a scope clip. Use two channels with
   both ground clips on GND, then CH1-CH2 across the coil, or a differential probe.
   Use insulated fine probes to avoid bridging adjacent pads.
6. Power on, read zero current, select the connected axis, 10 ms burst.
   Select single-shot scope trigger, confirm bench checkbox, Run coil burst.
   Frequencies: X 4.5 kHz, Y 5.625 kHz, Z 6.75 kHz. Non-selected inputs
   are both low, so those DRV8837 outputs coast. All channels share nSLEEP.
   Source current monitor voltage is near rail/2 + current[A]*4 V/A.
   Compare actual waveform/current against UI sampled min/max. Zero amplitude
   may indicate an open coil, failed driver, wrong connection or bad measurement.
7. After passing single-axis tests, Capture with coils collects 256 samples
   per selected port with source active. Tone amplitudes are raw ADC counts,
   NOT millimetres. Repeat no-coil capture for noise comparison. Check clipping.

STOP / LIMITATIONS
Stop all outputs sends an out-of-band ! byte, then requests sensor power off.
USB suspend, DTR drop, error and software timeout disable bridge sleep enable.
A separate independent watchdog resets a stalled MCU (nominally about 1 s;
LSI tolerance means this is not an exact maximum). GPIO reset/pulldown disables TX.
Burst request 1-100 ms, with 2 s cooldown; transmitter capture uses 50 ms ceiling.
A SysTick cutoff runs independently of the normal command loop; these timing
paths are software-tested, not measured on your hardware.
Current monitoring uses PA0 ADC1_IN1, PA1 ADC1_IN2, PA4 ADC2_IN17,
INA240A1 gain20 and 0.2 ohm shunts. It calibrates MCU ADCs and measures VDDA
against factory VREFINT calibration. Baseline is measured before each burst.
Selected channel guard 125 mA; other channels 25 mA. Guard is sampled and
software-based, NOT a certified hardware peak current limiter. A delayed
monitor (>5 ms), DMA error, ADC error or implausible baseline stops the burst.
47 ohm 1 W series resistors limit load current; at 5.25 V and 1% low resistance,
ideal short-circuit bound is about 113 mA and resistor power about 0.59 W/axis.
Coil resistance and driver losses lower current. No active temperature sensing.
Do not bypass the series resistors or apply an external higher voltage.

Physical checks still required: actual waveform timing, current calibration,
polarity, receiver response, ADC continuity under DMA load, USB disconnect
shutdown and temperature. This release has no continuous 100 Hz pose solver,
full geometric calibration or verified 1 mm accuracy. Do not call it a complete
motion tracking product merely because diagnostics pass.
