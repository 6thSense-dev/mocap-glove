Teach the glove its coordinates

Experimental single-fingertip tracking. Accuracy is not yet verified.

1 · Keep the board facing the same way

Keep the transmitter fixed. Define the center of its three windings as (0, 0, 0). Use its three perpendicular coil directions as the coordinate axes: X right, Y forward, Z up. The wire labels may differ; calibration will test their order.

Hold the fingertip board flat, component side up, with the flex tail always pointing the same way. This teaching orientation becomes zero rotation. Move its receiver coil center to each position below. Use a ruler or jig; do not bend or rotate the receiver island between reference captures.

Enter the actual measured X/Y/Z offsets in millimetres. The positions in the table are suggested targets, not measurements. If the flex cannot reach one, choose another position at a different direction and height.

2 · Capture eight reference positions

For each row, position the receiver, enter the ruler measurements, and capture. Each capture uses a short burst with all three transmitters. Keep everything still until it finishes.

3 · Check a new position and rotation

After calibration, move to a different position on the same side of the transmitter as reference 1 and take a reading. Compare it with a ruler. You can then rotate the fingertip board. Reference 1 selects the working side of the transmitter. Stay in that half of the workspace: this model cannot distinguish opposite hemispheres.
